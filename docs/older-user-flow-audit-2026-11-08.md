# HH ease-of-use audit — November 8, 2026

Baseline: live HH commit dcf9ffe8ca235df2b61a290e2b2c79668f10cf88 and identical input-card source on TEST.

This audit combines source review, synthetic task journeys, and additional measurements of text size, controls, contrast candidates and 200% text enlargement. Synthetic requests remain inside the local fixture server. No real household records or emails are changed.

The measurements are screening evidence, not accessibility certification or a substitute for sessions with older participants. Small controls can have applicable WCAG exceptions; contrast candidates require visual review, including states and backgrounds. Text enlargement is simulated per element, not a claim of physical iPhone testing.

## Areas under review

- Find settings and help from mobile navigation.
- Read instructions and operate controls without relying on icon recognition.
- Create, correct and recover household invitations.
- Add, edit, complete and restore groceries and to-dos.
- Create events, change time/date, repeat and select family members.
- Plan meals, save favourites, add ingredients and distinguish the saved meal from its planned copy.
- Find and edit family notes; search across sections.
- Understand saving, offline changes, failures and conflicting edits.
- Keep or discard unfinished entries consistently.
- Read and operate at increased text size.

Browser evidence is stored in the senior-audit branch's workflow artifacts in journeys.json and senior-*-text200 screenshots.

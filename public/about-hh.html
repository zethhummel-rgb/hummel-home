About HH

Hi, I’m Zeth. I built HH Family to make it easier for everyone at home to know what’s going on. When hockey practice is on the calendar, dinner is in a text thread, and the grocery list is on the fridge, it’s easy to miss something. HH gives the whole household one place to find it.

I’m inviting families to try HH as part of a pilot. I’d love to hear what makes your week easier and where the app could do better.

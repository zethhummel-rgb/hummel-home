Maple House

Alex, Jamie, Riley and Casey are fictional family profiles. Your existing Hummel Home login opens this household. No new password is needed.

  1. Home: check today's plans and meals, upcoming items, countdowns, the activity banner and motivational message.
  2. Calendar: open and edit timed, all-day, multi-day and recurring events. Change one occurrence, cancel one occurrence, and check people colours, searches and week navigation.
  3. Groceries: switch Store/Department, filter stores, search, edit quantities and units, add staples, mark items picked up and reopen them. Reopen the app and check the saved screen and scroll position.
  4. To Do: complete one-off and repeating tasks, check overdue/today/future items and the next due date. Edit a monthly recurrence near the end of a month.
  5. Meals: use all four meal types, multiple entries, favourites, copy/move plans and shopping for one meal/day/week. Check fractional quantities and ingredient deduplication.
  6. Notes: create, search, pin and edit notes. Open the same note twice to test stale-edit handling.
  7. Family/settings: change profile colours and palettes, replay the tutorial, create/revoke an invitation using an example.invalid address, and inspect the activity inbox.
  8. Send Feedback: open More or Settings, select an HH area or New Feature Suggestion, and enter a comment. Feedback goes only to hummelhomeapp@gmail.com with a TEST subject once the test sender is connected. Until then, the form shows that sending is temporarily unavailable.

External delivery tests are separate

Google Calendar copy/import, invitation email sending and device push delivery are deliberately disabled. Reminder fields and recurring schedules still work. Sign-up confirmation, password-reset emails, real multi-account sign-in, and physical iPhone push require a controlled follow-up test. Do not enter real invitees or connect a personal calendar.
